Privacy Policy

App: Smart Fridge & Home Inventory  ·  Developer: MHI SOFT LLC  ·  Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Smart Fridge & Home Inventory app ("Fridge," "the app," "we," "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Fridge is a personal food-inventory app. You can track what is in your refrigerators, freezers, and other spaces, scan product barcodes, and optionally share an inventory with members of your household. Using the app without sharing is fully local to your device and free; household sharing is a paid subscription.

1. Information we collect

We only collect data that you provide or that is required to make the app work. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking of any kind, and we do not sell your data or share it with data brokers.

a. Information you provide

  • Your name (display name). An optional name you enter so household members can see who added or changed an item. If you share a household, this name is visible to the other members of that household.
  • Inventory content. The names, quantities, units, categories, dish tags, store names, dates, and notes you record for your items and spaces.
  • Photos. Photos you choose to attach to items or spaces, taken with the camera or picked from your photo library.

b. Information collected to operate the service

  • Account identifier. When you use household sharing, the app signs you in through Firebase Authentication. By default this is an anonymous account identified only by a randomly generated user ID. You may optionally link Sign in with Apple so your household can be restored on a new device; in that case we receive the name and email address you authorize Apple to share (which may be a private Apple relay email). We use this only to identify your account and restore your data.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe to household sharing, we record whether your household's subscription is active. Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple — we never receive or store your card or bank details.

c. Barcode lookups

When you scan a product barcode, the barcode number is sent to the third-party Open Food Facts service to look up product details. The barcode number is not linked to your identity in that request. See Open Food Facts' privacy practices at world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your precise or coarse location, contacts, health or fitness data, financial data beyond subscription status, browsing or search history, device advertising identifiers, or diagnostic/usage analytics.

2. How we use your information

We use the information above solely to:

  • provide core app functionality (storing and displaying your inventory);
  • sync your data across the devices of your household, if you enable sharing;
  • identify household members and show who last edited an item;
  • restore your household on a new device when you sign in with Apple;
  • verify and maintain your subscription.

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or tracking.

3. How your data is stored and shared

  • Local-only use. If you do not enable household sharing, your data stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Household sharing (subscribers). When sharing is enabled, your inventory data and photos are stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) and synced to the devices of the members of your household. Only members of your household can read that data.
  • Service providers. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions) to provide the sync and sharing features, and Open Food Facts for barcode lookups. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.
  • We do not sell your personal data or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Data retention and deletion

  • Deleted items. When you delete an item or space, it is marked as deleted and removed from view immediately, then permanently purged from our servers after a short grace period (currently about 5 days) that allows the deletion to sync across devices.
  • Local data. Data held only on your device is removed when you delete the item, leave a household, or uninstall the app.
  • Account/household deletion. You can request deletion of your account and any household data stored on our servers by emailing us at the address below. We will delete the associated data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Sharing is optional. You can use the app fully locally without creating any server-stored data.
  • Sign in with Apple is optional. You can use the app without linking an Apple ID.
  • Photos are optional. You control which photos, if any, you attach.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data (for example under the GDPR or CCPA). To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

6. Children's privacy

Fridge is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We rely on Google Firebase's infrastructure and security rules to protect data in transit and at rest, and we restrict access to a household's data to that household's members. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International transfers

Your data may be processed and stored on servers operated by our service providers in the United States and other countries. By using the app you consent to this processing.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

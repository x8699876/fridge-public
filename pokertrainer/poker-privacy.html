Privacy Policy

App: Poker Trainer: Play & Improve  ·  Developer: MHI SOFT LLC  ·  Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Poker Trainer: Play & Improve app ("Poker Trainer," "the app," "we," "us") handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Poker Trainer is a single-player, play-money Texas Hold'em practice app. You play against computer opponents to improve your game. There is no real-money gambling, no cash-out, and no purchasable chips. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers operated by us.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect your personal data. We do not operate any server that receives your data, and we have no ability to see your play history, statistics, settings, or any other information you create in the app. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking, or advertising identifiers, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

All of your app data — your chip bankroll, game settings, hand history, session statistics, and in-progress tables — is created and stored on your own device.

2. iCloud sync across your devices

Poker Trainer can sync your data across the Apple devices signed in to your own Apple ID (for example, so you can start on your Mac and continue on your iPhone). This uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit private database.

  • It is your iCloud, not ours. Synced data is stored in your personal iCloud account under your Apple ID. It is not sent to MHI SOFT LLC, and we cannot read it. Apple manages this storage under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • What syncs. Your settings, chip bankroll, hand history and session statistics, and — if you resume a game on another device — the state of your in-progress table.
  • Single user, not sharing. Sync is only between your own devices. Poker Trainer has no household or multi-person sharing; other people never see your data.
  • Optional. If you are signed out of iCloud, or turn iCloud off for the app, Poker Trainer works fully on-device and syncs nothing.

3. Purchases

Poker Trainer offers a one-time "Pro" unlock. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never receive or store your card, bank, or payment details. Whether you have unlocked Pro is managed by Apple's purchase system (and restored across your devices by Apple), not by any server of ours.

4. Network connections

Apart from iCloud sync and Apple's purchase handling described above, the app makes one minor network request: to give computer opponents varied display names, it fetches a short list of random first names from the public randomuser.me service. This request sends no information about you — it is a generic request for sample names — and the app works normally (using a built-in list of names) if it is offline or the request fails.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data. Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Delete my synced data (in-app). Because synced data lives in your iCloud account, uninstalling the app does not remove the copy held in iCloud or on your other devices. You can erase it at any time using Settings → Delete My Synced Data in the app, which:
    • permanently removes your Poker Trainer data from your iCloud account (settings, bankroll, hand history, session statistics, and saved table state);
    • propagates that deletion to your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID; and
    • optionally clears the copy held locally on the current device.
    This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Your Pro purchase is managed by Apple and is not affected by deleting your data.
  • Removing iCloud data directly. You can also remove the app's iCloud data from any device under Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Sync is optional. Use the app fully on-device by keeping iCloud off for Poker Trainer.
  • Delete your data anytime. Use Settings → Delete My Synced Data (see Section 5).
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. Because we hold none of your personal data, those requests are satisfied by deleting your data yourself as described above; for any other question, contact us at the address below.

7. Children's privacy

Poker Trainer is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). The app depicts simulated, play-money card play and collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, note the change in the app. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

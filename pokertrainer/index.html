⭐ Real cards. Real deals. Nothing rigged.

PokerTrainer deals a real, fair deck, exactly like a casino table. No fake cards, no "engineered" bad beats, no hot streaks staged to keep you playing.

Don't take our word for it. Check it. Turn on God Mode → Reveal all hands and you can see every player's hole cards, the burned cards, and the entire remaining deck in order. What you see is what gets dealt.

  1. A standard 52-card deck, shuffled once per hand with Apple's cryptographically secure random number generator.
  2. Dealt in order from the top: hole cards, then burn + flop, burn + turn, burn + river, just like a real dealer.
  3. The AI plays fair too. Opponents only see their own two cards. They read the board and your betting, the same as a human would.
  4. Your results reflect your decisions, so what you learn here carries over to real games: pot odds, equity and outs work exactly as they do at a casino.

FAQ

Are the cards really random?

Yes. Each hand uses a fresh 52-card deck shuffled with Apple's secure random number generator and dealt in order, with burn cards. Turn on God Mode → Reveal all hands to see the whole deck and confirm the board runs out exactly as stacked.

Can the AI see my cards?

No. Each opponent only knows its own two cards, the board, and the betting.

Is this real-money gambling?

No. PokerTrainer uses play money only. There are no chips to buy and nothing to cash out. It's a place to enjoy the game and sharpen the skills you take to your own games.

How does the free trial work?

Your first 900 Hero hands unlock every feature except the Pro-exclusive Elite profile, which stays off and disabled. A remaining-hands counter keeps you informed. Every 100 hands, a dismissible reminder offers Pro; after hand 900, one trial-ended prompt replaces the regular reminder and your state becomes Free. Keep playing Free forever, or purchase Pro at any time. There is no automatic charge.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase that works on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, unlocks all features including Elite, and includes all future Pro feature updates for free. The purchase screen provides Restore Purchases, Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy links.

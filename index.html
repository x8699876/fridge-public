Smart Fridge

A Shared Storage Manager for Households, Clubs & Teams — iPhone & macOS

x8699876.github.io/fridge-public

Fridge Inventory Manager is a refrigerator/freezer and general household space inventory manager — covering pantries, counters, cabinets, closets, and any other storage space. See what's in every space at a glance, organize items by category, tag them by dish, and keep shopping and wish lists.

Reminders

Never let food spoil or a filter go unchanged again. Set expiry dates and replacement schedules for anything — groceries, water filters, pantry staples — and get notified before it's too late.

Sharing

Invite your household and sync every space live across everyone's devices. One shared inventory means no duplicate groceries, no forgotten items, and no "did anyone buy milk?" texts.

Beyond the household

Clubs, teams, and shared spaces get their own inventories too — a single source of truth for shared kitchens, storage rooms, and supply closets.

Know exactly what's in your fridge, freezer, and pantry — before you shop. Track food, cut waste, and keep every space organized.

How it helps your household

  • Find what's buried in the freezer. Put a numbered label (e.g. #12) on a bag or container, record the same number in the app, and jump straight to it instead of digging.
  • Shop smarter. Check what you already have — and what's running low — before you leave, so you don't re-buy things or forget the essentials.
  • Use it before it's wasted. Track added/expiry dates; items are flagged and sorted by what to eat first, cutting spoilage and waste.
  • Get reminded before it matters. Set reminders tied to any item's expiry or a custom date and get a local push notification on your iPhone — eat the yogurt before it turns or use up that face lotion before it expires. Fridge nudges you in time, not after.
  • Stay on top of home upkeep. Track recurring household chores the same way you track food — get reminded to replace the AC/furnace air filter, swap smoke-detector batteries, change the water pitcher filter, or rotate seasonal supplies — so routine maintenance never slips your mind.
  • Keep the whole family in sync. Everyone in the household sees the same spaces and items live — one person adds groceries, everyone's app updates.
  • Build the grocery run together. Anyone can start a shopping or wish list ("Bella's wishlist"), add items, and check them off at the store as a shared, real-time list.
  • Cook from what you have. Tag items by dish so you can see which meals you can make with what's on hand.
  • Decide what to eat today. Keep track of your dish menus and recipes, and browse them for inspiration when no one can decide on dinner.
  • Organize beyond the fridge. Track the pantry, counter, cabinets, and the closet — even which shoes are in which box — all in one place.
  • Never lose your data. Link an Apple ID so your household can be restored on a new or replaced phone.
  • Go beyond the household. The same shared, real-time spaces work for any group you're part of — pool gear and supplies with your hiking or book club, share notes and photos of a community garden harvest, coordinate a classroom's shared bins, keep a workshop's tools and parts organized with your team, or track consumables at a small café. If a group needs to see and update the same list together, Fridge can hold it.

Features

  • Shared spaces — every space is shared live with your whole group — a household, club, team, or any circle — so everyone sees the same items as they're added, updated, or used up. The owner invites members to join and can remove them at any time.
  • Spaces & sections — model fridges, freezers, and general storage (pantry, counter, cabinets…) and the sections within them.
  • Items — name, quantity/unit, photo, category, dish tags, dates, expiry. Items now have types, so Fridge handles more than food — track shoes, clothing, tools, cosmetics, and other general goods alongside groceries.
  • Reminders — schedule expiry and maintenance reminders per item (food freshness, water/air filter changes, cosmetics shelf life), delivered as local push notifications on your iPhone so nothing slips past its date.
  • Categories & dish tags — built-in (seeded) plus custom; categories are organized by item type so each type gets its own relevant set.
  • Shopping / wish lists — per-household lists; add items, check off purchases (checked hidden by default), sort, edit, creator/empty delete rules.
  • Tap to link Apple ID — link an Apple ID so a solo household can be restored on a new device.
